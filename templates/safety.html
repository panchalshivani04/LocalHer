Safety & Moderation Guidelines

LocalHer is committed to providing a safe, respectful environment for local women entrepreneurs and customers.

1. Personal Blocking (Level 1)

Any user can block another user specifically for themselves in chat. Once blocked, the other party cannot send messages or start new conversations with you.

2. Reporting Violations (Level 2 Moderation)

If you encounter harassment, spam, scams, or inappropriate behavior, submit a report via the chat menu. Reports are reviewed by administrators who take action including warnings, account suspensions, or permanent blocks.

3. Media Protection

All images shared in chat are protected and accessible only to conversation participants. Voice recordings are permanently disabled for safety and privacy.

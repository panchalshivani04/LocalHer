Privacy & Data Security Policy

At LocalHer, your privacy, safety, and confidentiality are our highest priorities. We enforce strict data minimization principles to ensure that customer and seller information is protected at all times.

1. Data Minimization

We collect only essential data required to facilitate local order requests and in-app communication. We do not store unnecessary personal details, voice recordings, or third-party tracking scripts.

2. In-App Chat Security

All chat messages and photo attachments are private between the conversation participants. Media files are stored securely and served only through server-authenticated endpoints. Voice messaging features are completely disabled for data privacy.

3. Unsend & Instant Data Deletion

Users have full control to unsend their messages or delete conversation threads. Deleted messages are removed immediately from the system database and synced real-time.

4. User Safety & Moderation

Users can block or report inappropriate behavior at any time. Reports are reviewed by platform administrators to keep the community safe for local women entrepreneurs and customers.
